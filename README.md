# 🍓 Berry — Learn. Grow. Thrive.

**Berry is a personalized, interactive learning platform designed to make education more accessible, engaging, and effective — from early childhood learning to university education and professional development.**

Berry aims to transform the way people learn by combining structured learning pathways, curriculum-aligned courses, interactive lessons, adaptive assessments, AI-powered learning assistance, and a supportive educational community into one connected platform.

Rather than treating education as a one-size-fits-all experience, Berry is designed around the individual learner: their educational stage, grade or qualification, learning objectives, progress, and personal goals.

## 🌱 Our Vision

To create an intelligent, inclusive, and engaging learning ecosystem where every learner can discover knowledge, develop practical skills, build confidence, and progress at their own pace.

## 🎯 What Is Berry?

Berry is built around a simple principle: **learning should be personalized, structured, interactive, and focused on genuine understanding.**

Learners should be able to identify exactly what they need to study, access relevant learning materials, practice what they learn, assess their understanding, and identify what to improve next.

The platform is organized into four learning experiences, each designed for a different stage of the educational journey.

## 📚 The Four Berry Learning Stages

### 🧸 Berry Ali — Early Childhood Learning

Designed for **PP1 and PP2**, Berry Ali introduces young children to learning through colorful visuals, playful interactions, simple explanations, games, and age-appropriate activities.

The experience focuses on making foundational learning enjoyable, accessible, and easy for young learners to understand.

### 🚀 Berry Junior — Primary and Junior School

Designed for **Grades 1–9**, Berry Junior supports learners through lower primary, upper primary, and junior school.

It aims to organize learning according to the Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), presenting the relevant subjects and learning content for each selected grade.

### 🎓 Berry Senior — Senior School

Designed for **Grades 10–12**, Berry Senior provides a more mature learning experience centered on grade-specific subjects, learning pathways, structured lessons, revision, assessments, and academic progress.

Its content is intended to reflect the learner's selected curriculum and applicable senior-school pathway.

### 💼 Berry Advanced — University and Professional Learning

Berry Advanced brings together university-level education and professional development.

It is designed to support university and college learners, postgraduate students, working professionals, job seekers, and lifelong learners through structured courses, practical learning, academic resources, and career-oriented development.

## ✨ Key Features

Berry is being developed around the following core capabilities:

- **Personalized learning:** Learning experiences tailored to the user's selected stage, grade, curriculum, qualification, and goals.
- **Structured courses:** Subjects organized into chapters, lessons, activities, assignments, chapter quizzes, and final course examinations where applicable.
- **Interactive learning:** Learning materials and activities designed to encourage active participation rather than passive content consumption.
- **Mastery-based progression:** A learning approach that emphasizes demonstrated understanding, meaningful feedback, revision, and improvement.
- **Explore Courses:** A course discovery experience that helps learners find relevant subjects and learning opportunities.
- **My Learning:** A personalized space for accessing enrolled subjects, navigating chapters and lessons, and tracking learning progress.
- **Berry AI:** An AI-powered learning assistant designed to explain concepts, guide problem-solving, and recommend relevant learning activities.
- **Berry Library:** A central place to discover educational resources, reference materials, revision resources, and other permitted learning materials.
- **Berry Community:** A learning-focused space for publishing educational blogs, sharing ideas, commenting, reacting to posts, and engaging with other learners.
- **Assignments and assessments:** Tools for completing activities, attempting quizzes, taking examinations, and reviewing feedback.
- **Progress tracking:** Insights into course completion, assessment performance, learning objectives, and areas requiring further practice.
- **Achievements and certifications:** A dedicated area for viewing earned achievements and certificates supported by the platform.
- **Calendar and notifications:** Tools for organizing learning activities, tracking deadlines, and receiving relevant updates.
- **Live transcription:** A learning and productivity capability intended for Berry Advanced university and professional learners.

## 🇰🇪 Curriculum-Centered, Globally Inclusive

Berry uses the Kenyan CBC as its foundational school-learning pathway for learners studying in Kenya, while aiming to support additional curricula and educational systems as the platform evolves.

The goal is to ensure that learners see content relevant to their actual grade or qualification rather than being presented with an unrelated mixture of subjects.

The broader vision is to make Berry adaptable to learners across different countries, educational systems, academic disciplines, and professional fields.

## 🧠 How Learning Works

Berry is designed around a structured learning hierarchy:

**Learning Stage → Grade or Qualification → Subject → Chapter → Lesson → Activity → Assignment → Chapter Quiz → Course Examination**

A learner selects their educational stage and specific grade or qualification. Berry then presents the relevant subjects or courses. Within each subject, the learner can explore its chapters, study individual lessons, complete activities and assignments, take chapter quizzes, and work toward the final course examination.

The learning experience is intended to help learners identify knowledge gaps, revisit challenging concepts, and strengthen their understanding before progressing.

## 🛠️ Technology and Development

Berry is an evolving software project focused on educational technology, interactive user experiences, personalized learning, and scalable application architecture.

The implementation uses the technologies and services configured in the project repository. The long-term technical direction emphasizes:

- Maintainable and reusable components.
- Responsive, accessible interfaces.
- Structured curriculum and course data.
- Reliable authentication and user permissions.
- Persistent learning progress and assessment records.
- Secure integrations with AI and other supported services.
- Scalable content management and future feature expansion.

The repository's actual dependencies, architecture, and available integrations should be treated as the source of truth for the current implementation.

## 🚧 Project Status

Berry is an actively evolving project. Its features, curriculum coverage, integrations, and learning workflows may be at different stages of implementation.

The long-term objective is to develop a fully functional learning platform in which the interface, course content, assessments, learner progress, community features, and supporting services work together reliably.

## 🌍 The Bigger Picture

Education should not stop at the classroom, and learning should not look the same for everyone.

Berry aims to connect foundational education, academic development, practical skills, and lifelong learning through one adaptable platform.

Whether a child is learning their first numbers, a secondary school student is preparing for examinations, a university student is studying a degree, or a professional is developing new skills, Berry's vision is to provide a structured and supportive environment for continued growth.

**Berry — where every learning journey has room to grow.** 🍓

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/)
- npm
- A Gemini API key, if required by the application

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone YOUR_REPOSITORY_URL
   cd YOUR_PROJECT_FOLDER
   ```

2. Install dependencies:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. Configure your environment variables in `.env.local`:
   ```env
   GEMINI_API_KEY=your_api_key_here
   ```

4. Start the development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

5. Open the local URL displayed in your terminal.
